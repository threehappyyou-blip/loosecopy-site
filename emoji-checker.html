Are your emoji helping or distracting?

A couple of well-placed emoji can add warmth without costing you anything. Past a certain point, though — especially one at the start of every single line, standing in for a bullet — a lot of LinkedIn audiences read it as unprofessional or spammy rather than friendly. This counts what you've actually got and flags the emoji-as-bullet-point pattern specifically.

"Light touch vs. heavy" is a judgment call repeated across most current LinkedIn writing advice, not a platform-enforced rule — see, e.g., this LinkedIn-expert breakdown of when emoji help vs. hurt. Treat the tiers below as a prompt to double-check, not a verdict.

Counts any standard pictographic emoji (faces, objects, symbols like 🚀 or 📈). Plain punctuation, numbers, and letter-like symbols (%, +, #) are not counted as emoji.
