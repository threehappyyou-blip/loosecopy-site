AI drafting tools write in markdown out of habit — **bold**, # headers, - bullets — because that's the format most of their training text used. Neither Twitter/X nor LinkedIn renders any of it: the symbols just sit there in the published post, looking like a typo. This finds the syntax that won't survive, plus placeholder text like [Your Name] or "Lorem ipsum" that sometimes slips through from notes or templates.
