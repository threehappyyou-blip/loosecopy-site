Policy

Retired the $9/mo plan. Loosecopy is free for everyone now — no paid tier, nothing gated behind a paywall — supported instead by optional Ko-fi donations and Google AdSense.

Added

A link-placement checker — flags a link left in the body of a post instead of the first comment.

Added

A hashtag checker — flags too many, too few, duplicates, or tags that are hard to read at a glance.

Added

A passive-voice checker — finds passive constructions and suggests the active version.

Fixed

Confirmed, by reading the actual code rather than just asserting it, that the drafting step runs on one system with no logging beyond what the data page describes — the "being confirmed" note there is gone now that it's checked.
