Which time did you mean?

Paste your draft and find two things that read differently depending on who's reading and when: a clock time like 3pm with no time zone attached, and relative-day words like "tomorrow" or "next Friday" that mean something different once a post is read later or shared somewhere else.

Flagged: "see you at 3pm", "the call starts at 9am", "tomorrow", "next Friday", "this weekend"  ·  Not flagged: "3pm EST", "10:00 UTC+1", "your local time", "March 4" (an actual date, not a relative one)

Clock times aren't the only date-ish thing that reads two ways — the date-format checker catches numeric dates like 3/4/2026 for the same reason. Back to the generator →

Heuristic, not a calendar: a clock time is flagged only when no time-zone abbreviation, UTC/GMT offset, or phrase like "your time" appears anywhere in the same sentence. Relative-day words are flagged every time they appear — most are completely fine for something meant to be read same-day, this is just a nudge to consider whether this post might be read later, shared, or seen by someone in another time zone.
