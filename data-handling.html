Where your text actually goes

The trust page is a promise about publishing behavior. This one is about data — checked against the real code where we could, and clearly marked where we're still double-checking. For the formal version, see the Privacy Policy →

Last checked: September 2026

What happens when you click "Generate drafts"

1

The text you pasted goes from your browser straight to our generation endpoint, over HTTPS.

2

That endpoint forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to actually write the Twitter thread and LinkedIn post.

3

The two drafts come back and land in your editor. Nothing is saved along the way — there's no "my past drafts" page, because there's no database keeping one.

What we do store

  • If you join the waitlist: your email address. That form is handled by Formspree, a third-party form service — they hold the submission, and we get a notification. We don't run our own database of emails outside of that.
  • Nothing else on our servers. There's still no login and no account — if you set up "Your voice" in the generator, that's saved in your own browser, not with us, and sent along with your text each time you generate, the same way the text itself is.
  • Same for the post journal — if you log what you posted, those entries are written to your browser's local storage only. There's no endpoint on our end that receives them.
  • Nothing from Google AdSense, which runs on this site now — Google and its ad partners may set their own cookies to serve ads, separate from anything listed here. Details and opt-out links are on the Privacy Policy.
  • Nothing from Ko-fi either — the "Support" link on every page sends you to Ko-fi's own site to donate if you choose to; we never see your payment details.

What we don't store

  • The text you paste in. It's sent to generate your drafts and isn't written to a database on our end.
  • Your generated drafts, once they're back in your browser. Close the tab and they're gone unless you copied them somewhere.
  • Any history of what you've generated before, since nothing above is tied to an account.

About the AI step specifically

Your text is processed by Anthropic's Claude API to write the drafts. Under Anthropic's standard commercial API terms, prompt content isn't retained by default and isn't used to train models without explicit permission.

The whole generation step runs on one system — a single Cloudflare Worker handles your request from the moment it leaves your browser, forwards it to Anthropic through Cloudflare's own AI Gateway, and hands the drafts back. We checked that code directly, line by line: it doesn't write your pasted text or the drafts anywhere — no log, no database, no file. The only things saved anywhere are the optional "voice" sample and post-journal entries you choose to save yourself, and those stay in your own browser as described above.

Source: platform.claude.com — API and data retention. Applies to standard commercial API usage; see the link for the full policy and any model-specific exceptions.
