Would this make sense outside your bubble?

"Move the needle" and "low-hanging fruit" are second nature once you're inside a company or industry — that's exactly why they're easy to leave in. Every reader who isn't already an insider has to stop and guess, and that goes double if English isn't their first language. This finds jargon, idioms, and acronyms you never spelled out, and suggests a plainer swap for each.
