Is a raw link sitting in your post?

LinkedIn keeps people on LinkedIn — its own team has said the algorithm doesn't prioritize posts with outbound links as much as native content. The common workaround is to leave the link out of the body and drop it in the first comment instead. This finds any URL sitting in your draft so you can decide where it belongs.

LinkedIn's reduced reach for posts with outbound links is confirmed by Hootsuite's 2026 breakdown of the LinkedIn algorithm. It's less clearly documented for X/Twitter — some creators report a similar effect, but there's no equivalent official confirmation, so this note is lower-confidence there.

This flags any URL-shaped text, whether or not it's actually going to LinkedIn — for a Twitter/X thread, a link in the body is far less of a concern. It also can't tell if a link is essential (like a signup link) versus optional; sometimes the link has to stay in the post, and that's a fine call to make.
