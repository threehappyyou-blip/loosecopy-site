Where did that number come from?

Finds any percentage, "X out of Y", or "Nx" claim in your draft and checks whether a source — a study, a survey, a link, an "according to" — sits next to it. This doesn't check whether the number is true; it just checks whether you said where it came from. That matters more than usual with AI drafts, which occasionally invent a statistic that sounds exactly right.

This only looks for a source-shaped phrase near each number ("according to," "study," "survey," a link, and similar) — it can't tell whether that source actually says what you claim, and it can miss attribution phrased in an unusual way. Treat a flag as "add a source" or "double-check this," not "this number is wrong."
