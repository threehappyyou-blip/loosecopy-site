Privacy Policy

The plain-language version and the actual policy are the same thing here — no separate "in simple terms" box hiding a denser real document underneath.

Last updated: September 2026
The short version
  • We collect your email only if you choose to join the waitlist.
  • We don't store the text you paste in, the drafts you generate, or any history of either — there's no account for any of it to attach to.
  • If you save an optional "voice" sample so drafts match your tone, that's stored in your browser only — never on our servers.
  • The post journal works the same way — if you log what you posted, those entries stay in your browser too.
  • If this site ever shows ads, the section below explains what that means for cookies.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you enter. That's handled entirely by Formspree, a third-party form service — they hold the submission and notify us; we don't run a separate database of emails ourselves.

That's the only information we intentionally collect right now. There's no account system, no login, and no user profile for anything else to be attached to.

What we don't collect

  • The blog post, notes, or any other text you paste into the generator
  • The Twitter thread or LinkedIn post drafts that come back
  • Any history of what you've generated — nothing is tied to an identity for this to attach to
  • Any "voice" writing sample you optionally save to help match your tone — that's kept in your browser, not collected by us
  • Anything you log in the post journal — the posts, notes, and outcomes you record there stay in your browser, not with us

Who else touches your data

Running Loosecopy means a few outside services are involved. Here's what each one sees:

  • Formspree — receives and stores your email address if you join the waitlist.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — receives the text you paste in, to generate your drafts. Under Anthropic's standard commercial API terms, prompt content isn't retained by default or used to train models without permission.
  • Vercel — hosts the server that forwards your text to the Claude API and returns the drafts to your browser.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website itself.

Source on the Anthropic point: platform.claude.com — API and data retention

Cookies & advertising

Loosecopy itself doesn't set tracking or analytics cookies today. If this site shows ads in the future — including through Google AdSense — Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings or, for participating third-party vendors generally, at aboutads.info.

Children's privacy

Loosecopy isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the link below and we'll remove it.

Your choices

Want your waitlist email removed, or have a question about any of this? Tell us here — the same form that routes bug reports goes to the one person who reads every submission, and this counts too.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, it gets a dated entry on the public changelog — the same place every other real change to this site gets logged, not a silent edit to this page alone.
