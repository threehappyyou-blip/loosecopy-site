We will never auto-publish for you.

That's the one rule everything else in Loosecopy is built around. Not a feature we haven't gotten to yet — a line we've drawn on purpose, because the moment a tool can post on your behalf without you reading it first, it stops being your voice.

This page is about publishing behavior. For what happens to your data specifically, see where your text actually goes → or the formal Privacy Policy →

What we will never build

  • A "connect your account" button that lets Loosecopy post on your behalf
  • Write-access to X, LinkedIn, or any other platform's API
  • Scheduled or automatic posting of anything generated here
  • A "publish now" button anywhere in this product — ever

What we always do instead

  • Every draft opens in a text box you can edit — not a preview, an editor
  • You have to actively check "I've read this and made it mine" before you can even copy it
  • Free tools that flag AI-sounding phrasing and hand you a disclosure line, before you post anywhere
  • You copy, you paste, you hit publish — on the platform's own screen, from your own account
