Is your post a wall of text?

On a phone screen especially, a long paragraph with no break in it reads as effort before a reader even starts — and a lot of people bounce right there. This splits your draft on blank lines, measures each resulting paragraph, and flags any chunk that's long enough to slow a reader down.

Short paragraphs with a blank line between them is near-universal LinkedIn formatting advice for 2026 — see, e.g., this LinkedIn post-formatting guide. The exact length that counts as "too long" is a judgment call, not a platform rule, so treat the flag here as a prompt to look, not a hard cutoff.

This checks paragraph breaks (blank lines), not sentence quality. If you paste your text as one giant block with no line breaks at all, everything counts as a single paragraph — that's the most common form of "wall of text" and gets flagged first.
