How much of this is passive voice?

"The feature was launched" reads differently than "we launched the feature" — the first hides who actually did it. AI drafts lean passive by habit, and it's a hard thing to catch in your own writing since it doesn't sound wrong, just softer. This looks for be-verb + participle patterns ("was written," "got shared," "has been reviewed") that usually mean passive voice, so you can decide where naming the "who" would land better.

Same detection approach (be-verb followed by a past participle, with a "by ..." phrase as a confidence signal) used by established tools like AnalyzeMyWriting's passive voice detector and the pattern Grammarly/Hemingway-style checkers are built on.

This is pattern-matching, not real grammar parsing — it can't reliably tell true passive voice ("was designed by the team") apart from a participial adjective ("was excited," "was tired"), so treat every highlight as a candidate to glance at, not a verdict. It also misses some real passives (contractions like "it's written" aren't caught) and passive voice isn't always wrong — sometimes the actor genuinely doesn't matter ("the site was down for an hour"). Use judgment, not a zero target.
