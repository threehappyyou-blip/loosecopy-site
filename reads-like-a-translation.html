Not a language test — a formality check. Twitter and LinkedIn have their own casual register, and slipping into textbook-formal English is the fastest way to read stiff, whoever wrote it.

This isn't detecting whether you're a native English speaker — plenty of native speakers write stiffly too, especially in a first draft, and plenty of second-language writers already write casually just fine. It's flagging one specific thing: formal-register habits (the kind taught as "correct" English) that read stiff specifically on casual platforms. Want to fix what it finds?
