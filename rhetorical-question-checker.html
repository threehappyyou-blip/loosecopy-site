Too many rhetorical questions?

Counts short tag-questions — "Sound familiar?", "Make sense?", "Crazy, right?" — used as a rhetorical device. This is a structural count, not a phrase list: any question five words or under is counted, so it catches tag-questions you didn't think to list, and it leaves longer, substantive questions alone since those read as genuine curiosity, not filler.

Flagged (5 words or fewer): "Sound familiar?", "Make sense?", "Still with me?", "Crazy, right?"  ·  Not flagged: "What's the best way to approach this particular problem?" (a real question, not a tag)

One short question on its own is completely normal — this only starts flagging at two, and calls it a pattern at three or more. It counts any question of five words or fewer, so it can occasionally catch a genuinely short real question ("Why though?") rather than a rhetorical tag — read the highlights rather than just trusting the count.
